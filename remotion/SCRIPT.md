## Why this works (and the previous cut didn't)

- **Every word names what's on screen.** "Hermes-4 decides" lands as the Hermes-4 card lights. Old script narrated facts you couldn't see.
- **Seraphina takes a deliberate silence.** Old script tried to overlap presenter + Seraphina. New script says "Listen." then shuts up.
- **Cuts the Problem comparison.** A demo doesn't need to argue against competitors — it needs to show what it does.
- **Cuts personal narrative ("I built it because…").** The viewer figures out who built it from context. Save the words for the proof.
- **60 seconds vs 100.** Twitter-friendly. No padding.
